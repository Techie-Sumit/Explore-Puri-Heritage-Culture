Infrastructure & Facilities

Modern amenities and well-developed infrastructure supporting both residents and the millions of pilgrims who visit Puri throughout the year.

Infrastructure Categories

Transportation

  • Puri Railway Station - Major terminus with 50+ daily trains
  • Well-connected bus network to all major cities
  • Auto-rickshaws and cycle-rickshaws for local transport
  • 60km from Biju Patnaik International Airport

Utilities

  • 24/7 electricity supply with backup generators
  • Municipal water supply with overhead tanks
  • Underground drainage system in main areas
  • Waste management and recycling programs

Healthcare

  • District Headquarters Hospital with 200+ beds
  • Multiple private clinics and nursing homes
  • Ayurvedic treatment centers and dispensaries
  • Emergency services and ambulance facility

Education

  • Gopabandhu Ayurveda College
  • SCS Autonomous College
  • Multiple government and private schools
  • Adult literacy and skill development programs

Markets & Commerce

  • Swargadwar Market, Temple Bazar, and Grand Road shopping
  • Traditional Handicrafts and Religious Items
  • Local Textiles and Street Food
  • Tourist-friendly shopping areas

Digital Infrastructure

  • Growing connectivity and digital services
  • 4G/5G Networks and Digital Payment systems
  • Online Services and WiFi Hotspots
  • Smart city digital initiatives

Government Services

  • Municipal corporation offices and administrative centers
  • Collector Office and Police Stations
  • Post Offices and Banks & ATMs
  • Citizen service centers

Water Management

  • Comprehensive water supply and conservation systems
  • River Wells and Storage Tanks
  • Filtration Plants and Rainwater Harvesting
  • Sustainable water management practices

Smart City Development

Puri is undergoing significant modernization as part of India's Smart Cities Mission, with focus on sustainable tourism infrastructure and improved quality of life.
